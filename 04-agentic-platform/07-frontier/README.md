# What happens when the loop has to deliver?

**Question:** stages 00–06 measured the code-fixing loop on a task set. This
stage asks the question the loop's existence implies: the same mechanisms —
retry, grounding, verification, permission ladders, gates — are what every
agentic surface that transacts now runs on. Search, ads, commerce, and risk
control are converging on intent-to-delivery as their decision surface, per
the [paradigm survey](../../reference/research/agentic-paradigm-restructuring.md),
and the pattern that survey documents is this mission's harness at industrial
scale. The four chapters below are the frontier reading of the loop this
topic built, each grounded in the mission's own recorded runs and in dated
external evidence.

**Before this:** [stage 06](../06-closing-the-loop/), which closes the
measured arc; the four chapters here are the decision-oriented reading of
everything stages 00–06 recorded.

## The frontier, as one causal chain

| Chapter | The question it answers | What it returns |
|---|---|---|
| [Where does intent stop being delivered?](intent-to-delivery/) | how often a stakeholder intent actually arrives, and where it stops | the produced-vs-delivered gap, read from the recorded arms (4/18 blind vs 18/18 harness) |
| [What does the software around the model own?](harness-anatomy/) | what a harness's control plane and compute plane each own | a control-plane audit table, and a way to read Claude Code, Codex, and Antigravity as the same five decisions |
| [What does a governed agent actually do?](control-plane-governance/) | which gates stop real failures and what they cost | a reconciliation gate read (14/18 blind calls rejected before delivery), priced against the harness |
| [When should the agent act, and when should a human sign it?](what-a-reasonable-agentic-product-is/) | where the automate-versus-gate line sits and what evidence draws it | a routing table per tier, with per-delivered-outcome prices and the reversal-rate metric |

Each chapter follows the same contract the [depth audit](../../reference/standards/depth-audit.md)
applies to every stage: the failure mode is named before the fix, the case is
found in recorded data, the fix's trade is measured, the whole loop (not just
the model) is covered, nothing is written from memory, and the reader leaves
able to answer the operational version of the question. Every number in the
four chapters traces to a `runs/` entry in this mission or to a dated
external source; no model was called and no new run was executed to produce
them.

## What this stage does not prove

The frontier reading is an analysis of recorded and published evidence, not
a new run: the tables above reuse the mission's 54 real model calls (42
attempts plus 12 retries) and do not add a model call or a dollar of spend.
The external claims — ARC-AGI-3 harness settings, the Epoch AI scaffold
audit, rollback rates, Gartner's cancellation prediction, the payment rails,
the ad pricing shift — are 2026 snapshots with dates attached, and each
chapter's evidence boundary says which of them this repo could check.
