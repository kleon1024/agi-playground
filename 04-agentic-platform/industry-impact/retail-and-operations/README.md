# Where the outcome is measurable enough to automate

> Dated survey, 2026-08-14. Sources cited inline.

**Question:** retail, logistics, and customer service score high on outcome
measurability — inventory counts, delivery times, and resolution rates are
all verifiable. What does agentic adoption look like in the measurable
cell of the matrix?

## The deployments

**Customer service** — the highest-volume cell. Klarna's replacement-agent
experiment is the canonical case: measurable resolution, measurable cost,
and a documented human-oversight boundary ([six-patterns](https://anci.app/blog/six-patterns-that-worked-one-that-didnt-enterprise-agent-2026)).

**Supply chain and inventory** — agents handle replenishment decisions and
supply-chain monitoring, where the outcome (stock level, lead time) is
continuously measured.

**Operations** — HR scheduling, workforce agents, and documentation
verification run on the same shape: bounded task, measurable outcome,
review gate.

## The pattern

The measurable cell is where auto-merge-style autonomy concentrates —
the same risk-scored auto-merge the software industry runs. The mission's
routing decision is this pattern at mechanism scale: when "done" is
verifiable, autonomy can rise; when it is not, the gate stays.

## What this does not say

It does not claim customer service is fully automated — the measurable
core is, the judgment boundary is not. It maps the cell where autonomy
scales first.
