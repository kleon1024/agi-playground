# The human-signoff boundary in high-stakes professions

> Dated survey, 2026-08-14. Sources cited inline.

**Question:** healthcare and legal have the highest failure costs on the
adoption matrix, yet both have production agents. Where exactly does the
human-signoff boundary sit, and what makes those deployments work?

## The deployments

**Healthcare administration** — clinical documentation agents and claims
work run in production; one ABA claim-denial deployment held denials below
2% ([agentic use cases](https://agenticaiinstitute.org/the-hottest-agentic-ai-examples-and-use-cases-in-2026/)).
The boundary: documentation and claims are measurable and reviewable;
diagnosis is not.

**Legal** — A&O Shearman and Harvey launched agentic multi-step workflows
for document work ([six-patterns analysis](https://anci.app/blog/six-patterns-that-worked-one-that-didnt-enterprise-agent-2026)).
The boundary: drafting and search are agent-shaped; final advice carries a
human signature.

## The boundary pattern

Both industries use the same shape: agents do the measurable, high-volume
precursor work (documentation, drafting, claims, search), and humans sign
the consequential output. That is the authorization matrix's high-failure
cost row: low autonomy, explicit approval before any consequential action.

## What this means for this topic

The mission's "what-a-reasonable-agentic-product-is" chapter drew the same
line for software: the agent acts, the human signs. Healthcare and legal
are the same boundary in industries where the failure cost made the line
unavoidable.

## What this does not say

It does not claim the boundary is fixed — it moves as verification
improves. It maps where it currently sits in two high-stakes professions.
