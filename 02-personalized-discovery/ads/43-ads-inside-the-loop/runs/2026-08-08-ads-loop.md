## Purpose

The chapter's question is what survives of the auction when the ad becomes a
step in the answer thread. This run reads three committed records — the
ad-externality displacement model (stage 18), the value-tree auction entry
(stage 05), and the budget-pacing delivery simulation (stage 17) — and prints
the displacement table and pacing contrast the argument rests on.
