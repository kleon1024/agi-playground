# Which topic are you actually trying to understand?

The [build path](/playground/) reads one mission top to bottom. This page cuts
the other way: pick a decision you care about and follow every chapter that
touches it, in the order that makes the argument build, regardless of which
mission or platform area it lives in.

Each topic below names the chapters in the order to read them. A chapter that
appears in more than one topic is not duplicated content — it is one chapter
answering more than one question, and the topic list tells you which question
you are reading it for this time.
