# Which decision are you actually trying to make?

The sidebar groups chapters by who owns them: a mission owns an outcome and the
evidence for it, a foundation owns mechanism that holds regardless of which
mission you are running, infrastructure owns the machine underneath, and
reference owns contracts and dated survey material. That is the right way to
decide *where a new chapter belongs*. It is the wrong way to find something
when you already have a question.

This page cuts the other way. Pick the decision you are facing and read every
chapter that touches it, in the order that makes the argument build, no matter
which section it lives in. **Every published chapter appears here at least
once**, and a test fails the build if one stops appearing. A chapter listed
under two topics is not duplicated content — it is one chapter answering two
questions, and the topic heading tells you which one you are reading it for.

If you would rather start from a stakeholder problem than from a decision, the
last topic on this page lists the nine missions.
