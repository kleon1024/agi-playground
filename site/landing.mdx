## Know what the evidence proves

**Every capability claim is backed by a run. Every mission is backed by a
measurable outcome.** A verified label means the exact command, hardware,
wall-clock, cost, and metrics are recorded. A draft may contain working code,
but its declared path is not yet evidenced.

Business outcomes cannot be executed on a GPU. Missions therefore declare a
reproducible proxy, guardrails, and what the result does *not* establish before
the build begins.
