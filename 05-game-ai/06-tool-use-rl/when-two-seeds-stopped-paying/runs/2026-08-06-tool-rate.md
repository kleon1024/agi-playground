## Purpose

Stage 06's recorded verdict is 1/3 seeds calibrated, 2/3 collapsed. This run
reads the recorded per-step `tool_rate` — the fraction of completions that
paid for the tool — so the collapse is a trajectory, not a verdict.

## Notes

- The calibrated seed (0) keeps the tool alive across training and its rate
  oscillates (0.67 -> 0.10 -> 0.53) — the signature of a difficulty-
  conditioned policy that pays for the tool when the level asks for it and
  answers directly when it does not.
- The collapsed seeds (1, 2) die monotonically: 0.70 -> 0.00 -> 0.03 and
  0.55 -> 0.10 -> 0.00. They stop paying for the tool entirely and answer
  directly at every level, the context-independent collapse the recorded
  run's per-level breakdown names.
- The trajectory separates the two outcomes earlier than the final verdict:
  by mid-training seed 0 has already recovered its rate after a dip, while
  seeds 1-2 are at or near zero.
