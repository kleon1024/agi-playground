## Purpose

Measure the served per-speaker mix of `speech_data.build_dataset` (naive
speaker-major slice) against `multi_speaker_data.build_balanced_dataset`
(per-speaker bound) at the exact sizes the stages use. This is the
case-finding step for the "the mix is not what you asked for" failure mode:
count served speakers per split, never trust the requested list.

## Notes

- The naive builder slices the speaker-major utterance list, so the first
  40 utterances of a 10-speaker request are all from speaker 2277 (95
  archived utterances, first in the request order).
- Probe A reproduces stage 03's recorded call exactly (2 speakers,
  `max_utterances=40`) and shows both splits served speaker 2277 only: the
  stage's recorded "1-2 speakers" runs were a 1-speaker measurement.
- Probe B1 shows the loud failure at stage sizes raises with guidance that
  misdiagnoses the cause ("raise max_utterances or add a speaker" — the real
  cause is the slice, not volume). Probe B2 shows the completing failure
  serves one speaker with no warning.
- Probe C shows the balanced builder serves all 10 requested speakers in
  both splits, and raises (eval-coverage guard) rather than silently
  under-testing when a requested speaker is missing from eval.
