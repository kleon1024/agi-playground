## Purpose

The optimizer comparison ran SGD, momentum, and Adam on the same bowl
(A=100, B=1). This run reads the recorded JSON and lays out the mechanism:
step count and steep-axis sign-flip count move together.

## Output

```
bowl: A=100 B=1 condition number 100
  optimizer  steps  steep-axis flips  flips/steps
  sgd          343               341       0.99
  momentum     138                47       0.34
  adam          82                 4       0.05
```

## Notes

- The flip count is the direct measure of oscillation across the steep
  axis: SGD flips on almost every step (341/343), momentum damps it
  (47/138), Adam nearly removes it (4/82).
- Fewer flips is what makes fewer steps possible — the three optimizers
  converge in 343, 138, and 82 steps respectively.
