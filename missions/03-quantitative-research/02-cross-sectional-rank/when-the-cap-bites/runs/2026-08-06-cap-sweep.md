## Purpose

the universe once and measures the cap's three-way trade across five values

## Notes

- The cap binds only below 0.25 on this 30-name universe: beyond it every
  row is identical, because no name's natural weight exceeds the cap.
- Tighter caps cut concentration (rank HHI 0.109 to 0.032) and shrink gross
  exposure (1.45 to 0.79), but do not improve the paper Sharpe (rank -0.66
  at 0.25 to -0.76 at 0.05; signal -0.82 at 0.25 to -0.72 at 0.05). On this
  window the cap buys diversification at the cost of exposure, not return.
- The cap-violation count is a tightness tax: sector demeaning re-breaches
  the cap 102 times at 0.05, 49 at 0.10, zero at 0.25+ — the constraint
  pipeline's own artifact, measured rather than asserted.
- One fetch window (live endpoint, drifts between runs); Sharpe signs are
  this window's momentum regime and carry no claim beyond it.
