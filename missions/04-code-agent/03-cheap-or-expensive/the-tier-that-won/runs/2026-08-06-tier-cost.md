## Purpose

Stage 03's recorded run resolved all 18 tasks across all three tiers, so the
resolve rate separates nothing. This run lays out the per-tier economics
that remain: cost, tokens, turns, and wall-clock.

## Notes

- Haiku resolved the same 18/18 at 10.6% of the total cost — 0.1604 mean
  per task versus opus's 0.8226, about a fifth.
- The cheap tier did not get there for free in iteration: it produced more
  output tokens per task (7,513 vs 4,368-4,680) and took more turns (10.5 vs
  9.8-11.2) — it worked harder per task, at a fraction of the price.
- Sonnet sits between on cost (0.5369) but fastest on wall-clock (71s). The
  three tiers trade price against tokens-and-time differently; when resolve
  is saturated, the decision is which of those you are paying for.
