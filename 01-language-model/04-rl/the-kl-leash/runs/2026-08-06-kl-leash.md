## Notes

- k3 is always non-negative; the naive d goes negative, which is the
  sign-flipping gradient the estimator exists to remove.
- The asymmetry: at equal |d|, reducing probability mass costs more than
  increasing it — new/ref 0.5 gives k3 0.307 while new/ref 2.0 gives 0.193.
- The toll at beta 0.04 stays small across this range (max 0.045 at the
  extreme 0.3x), which is the leash's character: a soft constraint, not a
  hard wall.
