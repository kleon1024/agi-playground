# Run — 3.0B-token pretraining of the 88M model

The real run. [`2026-07-26-loop-mechanics.md`](2026-07-26-loop-mechanics.md)
verified the loop against a 1,024-vocabulary smoke tokenizer; this is the same
loop against stage 01's real tokenizer and the full corpus.

**Date:** 2026-07-27 to 2026-07-28
**Cost:** \$0 (local lane). 4.98 hours of GPU time.

## Command

```bash
python prepare_data.py ../data/fineweb-edu/sample/10BT ../stage01/tokenizer_hf.json \
  --out-dir data/tokens --val-tokens 5000000
python train.py --data data/tokens --out ckpt --tokens 3.0e9 --compile
```

## Hardware

RTX 4090 24GB (driver 591.86), reached over Tailscale SSH into WSL2.
Ubuntu 22.04.3 LTS, kernel 6.18.33.2-microsoft-standard-WSL2.

## Software

Python 3.12, torch 2.13.0+cu130, CUDA 13.0, in `~/agi-playground/.venv`.
The machine has nine other Python environments with torch installed, ranging
from 2.0.0+cu117 to 2.7.1+cu126; running this in the wrong one is the most
likely way to fail to reproduce it.

## Model

88,197,888 parameters, of which 75,516,672 are non-embedding. 12 layers,
`d_model` 768, 12 query heads, 4 KV heads, `d_ff` 2,048, block size 1,024,
tied embeddings. KV cache 12,288 bytes/token in bf16, against 36,864 under
full multi-head attention.

### The initialization check

Step 0 came out at 9.8697 against `ln(16,512) = 9.712`, the loss of a model
that assigns equal probability to every token. A randomly initialized model
must start there, because it has no information to do better with. Landing
0.158 above it is the expected small excess from initialized weights that are
random rather than exactly uniform in their effect.

This one number is worth more than it looks. Had it come out near 5, the head
would have been leaking information about the target. Near 12, something in
initialization or the loss reduction would be wrong. It is the cheapest
possible test that the data path, the model, and the loss agree with each
other, and it is available one step in rather than five hours in.

### The last 1,500 steps went the wrong way

Validation loss bottomed at 3.0689 (step 21,000) and finished at 3.0984
(step 22,500) — 0.0295 worse, over the final 6.5% of the budget, while the
learning rate was still decaying toward its floor. The run is reported at both
numbers rather than at the better one.

Three explanations are consistent with what was measured, and this run does not
separate them: the model is beginning to overfit as it approaches one full
epoch (0.95 epochs at the end); the cosine schedule's floor is not low enough
to settle; or it is evaluation noise, since each point is 50 iterations of a
5M-token validation set rather than the whole of it. Distinguishing them needs
a second run, which is exactly the multi-seed argument in
[`foundations/05-is-the-difference-real`](../../../foundations/05-is-the-difference-real/).

The checkpoint saved at the end is the step-22,888 checkpoint, not the
step-21,000 one. Downstream stages therefore start from the final weights, not
the best-scoring ones.

## Generations

Sampled from the final checkpoint at temperature 0.8, top-k 50, seed 0. Prompt
in bold.

> **The Industrial Revolution began in** the late 1800s, the United States was
> in the midst of a major technological revolution. The rapid growth of
> computers and the increased production of electronic circuits led to the
> emergence of the first electronic computer.

> **Photosynthesis is the process by which** the sun shines on the Earth, or in
> the atmosphere, to provide oxygen for a person to breathe. If the atmosphere
> is too dry or too dry, the oxygen can be lost by the wind, rain or snow fall.

> **The capital of France is** the city of Monaco, which is the largest city in
> the Mediterranean. It is the most populous European city in the Mediterranean,
> but also the fourth largest city in the Mediterranean.

This is the result, and it is the expected one. The model has learned English
morphology, syntax, register, and the shape of an encyclopedia paragraph. It
has not learned that the Industrial Revolution predates computers, that
photosynthesis is performed by plants, or that Monaco is not in France. At 88M
parameters and 3.0B tokens it was never going to. Fluency arrives early and
cheaply; factual grounding does not arrive at this scale at all.

Reported so that no reader mistakes a falling loss curve for a working model.

## Notes

- **The earlier ETA was 1.76x pessimistic.** It was projected from an
  uncompiled measurement. Enabling `--compile` moved throughput from 85.5k to
  165.6k tokens/second and MFU from 33.3% to 64.5%, turning a projected 9.8
  hours into an actual 4.98. Any wall-clock estimate carried over from a
  differently-configured run is a guess wearing a number.
- **A monitoring command silently dropped 95% of the log.**
  `grep -E '^step +[0-9]+ '` matched nothing past step 999, because the log
  comma-formats step numbers as `step   1,000`. The fix was
  `'^step +[0-9,]+ +val'`. This is the same class of error as the earlier
  `pgrep | head -1` that matched a wrapper shell and reported 0% CPU for a
  healthy job: the check ran, returned cleanly, and measured the wrong thing.
  A monitoring command that returns no rows should be treated as broken until
  proven empty.
- **Throughput rose slightly throughout**, from 150.6k tok/s at the first
  reading to 167.2k at the last. The early figures include compilation and
  warmup amortized over few steps; they are not a slowdown that later
  recovered.
- **Peak VRAM was 9.05 GB of 24 GB.** The run was not memory-bound, and a
  larger micro-batch was available. It was not taken, because the token budget
  and batch size were fixed before the run rather than tuned during it.
- The generations above were produced by a throwaway sampling script, not by
  anything committed in `core/`. Stage 05 is where serving and sampling become
  part of the lesson.

## What this run does not establish

That the architecture choices are good ones. Nothing here is a comparison —
there is no second arm, no ablation, and one seed. It establishes that this
configuration trains to this loss on this data in this time, and that the
resulting model is fluent and unreliable.
