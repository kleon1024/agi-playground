## Purpose

The observability chapter instrumented a training loop and recorded the
per-step time distribution. This run reads the JSON and lays out the
reading: the mean hides the tail.

## Output

```
steps=200 tokens=102400
  step time: p50 18.45ms  p95 21.14ms  mean 18.72ms  min 16.43ms  max 29.94ms
  max/mean ratio: 1.60x
```

## Notes

- The mean (18.72ms) is close to p50 (18.45ms) and hides the step that
  took 29.94ms — a 1.6x ratio that becomes a timeout under a real latency
  budget.
- p95 (21.14ms) captures the tail the mean cannot: this is why p95 exists
  as a metric and why the chapter's histogram, not its mean, is the
  evidence.
